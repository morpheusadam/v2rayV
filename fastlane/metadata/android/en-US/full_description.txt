<p>A free, open-source V2Ray / Xray client for Android, for people who use public subscription links and would rather have the client find a working server than tap through a list of dead ones.</p>

<h3>Auto Mode</h3>

<p>Most clients hand you three hundred servers and wish you luck. Most of them are dead, and some of the live ones are slower than your own connection.</p>

<p>v2rayV does that work itself. One button. It fetches your subscription sources, imports what it finds, throws out what cannot carry traffic, and measures what survives <b>against your own connection speed</b> — a server is good enough when it delivers at least 70% of what your bare line delivers. It connects on the first one that clears the bar, then keeps testing in the background so the next press is instant.</p>

<p>That comparison is the part no other client makes. Ranking servers against each other cannot tell you the fastest of ten bad servers is still a bad server, and on a 20 MB/s line a 1 MB/s "winner" is a failure.</p>

<h3>What it does while you are not looking</h3>

<ul>
<li>Keeps a reserve of ready, already-tested servers, so only the very first connection is ever slow.</li>
<li>Notices when the network under the tunnel changes — Wi-Fi to mobile data, a new address from your carrier — and checks the server still works there before leaving you on it.</li>
<li>Moves to the next server on its own when a connection stops carrying traffic.</li>
<li>Refreshes the reserve on a schedule, never while the tunnel is up, so a background refresh is not a background battery drain.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Protocols</h3>

<p>VLESS, REALITY, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2 and TUIC, on the Xray and v2fly cores.</p>

<h3>What it does not do</h3>

<ul>
<li>No account, no sign-up, no subscription to buy. It is a client: you bring your own servers or public lists.</li>
<li>No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting to anyone.</li>
<li>It does not sell you a VPN service and is not affiliated with any provider.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>

<ul>
<li>Installs alongside v2rayNG rather than replacing it — its own data, its own icon, its own notification.</li>
<li>Android 7.0 or newer.</li>
<li>Free software under the GPL-3.0. A fork of v2rayNG by 2dust, whose work the whole thing rests on.</li>
</ul>
